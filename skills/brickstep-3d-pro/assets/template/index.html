How to build

🖱️
Snap a brick

Click the glowing ghost, press Space, or drag the brick from the Bay onto the target.

🧭
Orbit & zoom

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan. Reset View recenters the build.

⟳
Rotate

Press R or use the Rotate button. In guided mode the snap auto-aligns to the target studs.

👁
Finished preview

Switch View Mode to Finished to see every piece without changing your saved step. Toggle back to Build to continue.

💥
Explode view

Pull the entire model apart along Y to inspect every layer. Return the slider to 0 for assembly.

✏️
Authoring studio

Enter Authoring Mode, pick a brick and color, then click the baseplate to stack. Up to 250 pieces. Export the step sequence as JSON.

💾
Save & load

Progress is auto-saved to this browser. Use Save Progress for a portable brickstep_pro_250.json file.
